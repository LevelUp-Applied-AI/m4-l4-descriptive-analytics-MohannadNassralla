# Exploratory Data Analysis Report
## Hypothesis Test Results
--- Hypothesis 1: Internship & GPA ---
T-statistic: 13.5644, P-value: 0.0000
Cohen's d: 0.6898
Result: Significant difference in GPA based on internship status.

--- Hypothesis 2: Scholarship & Department ---
Chi2: 13.9486, P-value: 0.3040, DOF: 12
Result: No significant association.


## Recommendations
1. **Expand Internship Programs**: Correlation shows positive impact on GPA.
2. **Targeted Support**: Departments with lower median GPA (see violin plot) need resources.
3. **Attendance Incentives**: High correlation between attendance and performance.
